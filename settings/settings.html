Moved over from Sidebery

When Firefox asks whether Tidier may hide tabs, choose Keep Tabs Hidden. Disable Extension would turn Tidier off.

Tidier may not hide tabs here yet, so Firefox's own tab bar and Ctrl+Tab still hold every space's tabs. The button under Spaces lets it; when Firefox then asks, choose Keep Tabs Hidden.

Spaces

A space is a Firefox container, with its own logins.

Without the next permission, a page's first request can go out in the wrong space before Tidier opens it again in the right one.

The same on every machine

Through Firefox Sync: this Firefox must be signed in to a Firefox account, with Add-ons ticked in its Sync settings. Ordinary tabs and what is open stay on each machine. When you turn this on, what is here and what your other machines have are put together, after a copy is made.

Rules

An address always opens in a given space. Text to find in the address, or /a regular expression/ (no look-around or back-references). Looked at for a tab's first page and an address you choose, never for a link inside a page or a redirect.

Tabs that tidy themselves

Essentials, tabs in folders, the tabs you are looking at and tabs playing sound stay. A closed tab is in its space's list of archived tabs.

Keys

Click a key, then press the new one. ⌫ removes it, Esc keeps it. ⌥⌘ and an arrow stay Firefox's. A key Firefox uses itself, such as ⌘1 or ⌘D, is dashed until it is cleared in about:keyboard.

A copy of your essentials and folders

They are kept nowhere else, so Tidier makes a copy once a day, and keeps those of the last two weeks in ~/.config/tidier/copies (its helper does that: native/install.sh). Taking one in adds what is missing here, and removes nothing; it can also bring another machine's spaces here.

Tidier

Tidier alone

This moves this browser from Sidebery to Tidier's own sidebar, spaces and folders. Nothing is closed until the record of it is written, and it can be undone.

    1. Disable Sidebery in about:addons. Keep it installed. (With it running, closing a folded folder's page would close the tabs in it.)

    To go back: enable Sidebery in about:addons first.

    Spaces

    A space is a Firefox container, with its own logins.

    The same on every machine

    Through Firefox Sync, which must have Add-ons turned on in its settings. Ordinary tabs and what is open stay on each machine. When you turn this on, what is here and what your other machines have are put together, after a copy is made.

    Rules

    An address always opens in a given space. Text to find in the address, or /a regular expression/ (no look-around or back-references). Looked at for a tab's first page and an address you choose, never for a link inside a page or a redirect.

    Tabs that tidy themselves

    Keys

    Click a key, then press the new one. ⌫ removes it, Esc keeps it. ⌥⌘ and an arrow stay Firefox's. A key it uses itself, such as ⌘1 or ⌘D, works once you clear it in about:keyboard.
